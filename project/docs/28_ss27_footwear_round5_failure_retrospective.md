# MOVERNO SS27 Footwear Round 5 — Failure Retrospective

> Decision date: 2026-09-03  
> Scope: `assets/ss27-footwear-family-expansion-round1/`  
> Status: all six concepts rejected

## What was misunderstood

“Other shoe silhouettes” was incorrectly interpreted as category diversification into derby, moc, mule and generalized outdoor shoes. In the established MOVERNO context, it means additional **fashion-sneaker silhouettes that remain close to the user's eleven visual references**: sculptural technical runners, dark Y2K runners, cage/wrap structures, fashion platform lows, bold court/skate lows and directional high-tops.

The rejected batch over-weighted market whitespace and mature manufacturing logic while under-weighting youth-fashion image impact. It therefore produced business-casual, comfort, medical and generic utility cues.

## Visible failure modes

1. Toe shapes were rounded, safe and mature instead of taut, flowing or assertively sculpted.
2. Side panels became large inert blocks; they looked attached rather than grown from the shoe's motion lines.
3. The derby and moc hybrids read as office or comfort footwear rather than trend products.
4. Buckles and straps created medical/orthopedic or harness associations.
5. The mule approached a garden/medical clog despite the written prohibition.
6. The trek runner returned to a generic black running-shoe template and overlapped existing families.
7. Manufacturability constraints were allowed to flatten the visual proposition too early.
8. Text-led prompts were not grounded by a one-to-one visual silhouette reference for every concept.

## Corrected design contract for the next round

- Reference first: every concept must be paired with an approved visual shoe reference before generation.
- Stay inside youth fashion sneakers; do not introduce derby, moccasin, mule, clog, medical, comfort-walking or office-casual categories unless explicitly requested.
- Silhouette fidelity first: preserve the reference's stance, toe length, collar height, sole mass and upper-to-sole ratio before adding MOVERNO assets.
- One structural intervention only: gate emblem as a regular side mark, Motion Contour as a support rail/panel/cage, or one controlled closure system.
- No oversized blank leather areas, conservative comfort soles, generic dress-shoe uppers or commodity trail-runner panel maps.
- Maintain the user's preferred palette: black, graphite, aged silver, bone and weathered sand; no decorative multicolor.
- Use exact standard emblem geometry where a visible logo is required. Do not ask the image model to invent a new logo.
- Generate one mother concept at a time after the silhouette reference is approved; stop after each reference family for review.

## Next confirmation gate

Curate a small reference board from the user's original eleven references plus current Japanese/Korean fashion-sneaker sources. The board must group visual directions by silhouette, not by market category. No new product render is generated until that board is approved.
