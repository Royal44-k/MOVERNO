## Why this round exists

The retained six-style group already covers chunky technical runners, a platform low, two high-tops and a skate low. This round fills use and silhouette gaps instead of adding more similar Y2K dad shoes: visually thin, moc hybrid, dark commute, ventilated summer, easy-on mule and reduced-volume light trek.

## Shared prompt contract

- Preserve MOVERNO's youthful, dark, quiet-luxury identity.
- Use only Obsidian, Graphite, Bone Ash and weathered sand; aged brass and dried-blood red together remain below 2%.
- EU42 visual sample, EU35–45 target range and broad-forefoot design goal.
- Mature outsole only; no proprietary outsole tooling or unsupported performance claims.
- One main brand action per shoe. The standard gate emblem cannot become a butterfly, shield or weapon; its center gap cannot close.
- Motion Contour must perform support, reinforcement, ventilation, flex or closure work; never a full-surface decorative print.
- No generated text, wordmark, third-party marks, signature brand panel maps, recognizable proprietary soles, extra shoes, feet, props or watermark.

## Preliminary visual QA before review

- All six are category-distinct and use a monochrome/low-saturation system.
- 1 successfully establishes the thin-family gap, though lace color and wing scale may need a later single-variable pass.
- 2 establishes the moc hybrid but its side gate should be simplified if selected.
- 3 is the cleanest commercial dark-commute proposal.
- 4 carries the strongest functional innovation; strap mechanics require prototype validation.
- 5 is intentionally polarizing and editorial; the side ventilation openings and rear fixture need further rationalization if selected.
- 6 provides the outdoor bridge but must be checked against the existing runner family so it remains visibly lighter.

## User review decision

All six concepts were rejected. The set drifted into business-casual, comfort, medical/utility and generic outdoor footwear, making the collection look older and less fashion-led than the user's reference set. None may proceed to revision, multi-view development, last/outsole sourcing or technical-pack work. The files remain only to prevent the same directions from being regenerated.
