## Final prompt set

All prompts classified the mother image as the edit target, the approved pattern as a supporting design reference, and the emblem proof as exact geometry reference. Shared constraints: preserve each shoe's silhouette and category; production-plausible materials and seams; no text, wordmark, third-party branding, extra shoes, feet, props, watermark, fantasy armor, or multicolor styling.

### 1

Deepen the sand chunky technical runner while preserving broad forefoot, runner proportions, sand/bone/graphite palette, segmented sole and lacing. Replace the oversized blank lateral suede zone with two opposing functional overlays around a ventilating mesh gate, using one enlarged Motion Contour unit rather than a repeat print. Use a small tone-on-tone heel emblem.

### 2

Deepen the black/graphite low technical cage runner while preserving its low profile, broad forefoot, mesh body and segmented sole. Rationalize the wrapping into five or six deliberate flat webbing load paths anchored at lace throat, quarter and midsole channels. The routes form opposing arcs and a narrow gate slit; remove loose crossings and snag points.

### 3

Deepen the black platform low-top while preserving black leather/suede upper, bone laces, metal eyelets and faceted bone platform. Reduce perceived platform bulk by about 10–12%, refine the toe, and translate Motion Contour into shallow molded sidewall facets with a recessed monochrome emblem at the protected arch.

### 4

Deepen the black high-top zipper sneaker while preserving ankle height, padded collar, black canvas/leather upper, bone laces and bone cupsole. Replace the hanging gold chain with one recessed oxidized-nickel zipper inside a narrow black gate slit. Use fine aged-brass seam piping only, and the emblem only on the zipper pull/heel application.

### 5

Deepen the black high-top while preserving its slim urban silhouette, bone cupsole/laces and vertical lateral motif. Rebuild the motif as two opposing articulated reinforcement pieces sewn into collar, heel counter and foxing, separated by a recessed flexible mesh channel and joined by one tiny dark-red stress node.

### 6

Deepen the black low skate/court shoe while preserving low collar, broad forefoot, flat bone cupsole, bone laces and black suede toe. Recut lateral patches into opposing Motion Contour abrasion layers: an elongated rear protector and smaller forefoot/ollie reinforcement meeting around a narrow gate slit, with all edges double-stitched.
