# MOVERNO footwear extension R07–R12 — Japan/Korea primary references

Research date: 2026-09-11 (Asia/Shanghai)

## Scope and evidence boundary

- This note is a narrow first-party product-page check, not a claim of comprehensive Japanese/Korean social-media coverage, market heat, or sales popularity.
- Only UGG Japan, adidas Korea/Japan and New Balance Japan first-party pages were used. No marketplace/editorial images are cited.
- Product-page text was accessible through web retrieval. The pages exposed direct official-CDN image URLs, but the sub-agent's in-app image viewer could not render them (sub-agent browser limitation / CDN timeout). Therefore the details below are explicitly separated into **official page statements** and **design translation**; no unverified visual trait is presented as personally observed.
- Main-agent visual review is still required before any image is placed on the final board.

### What the official page itself states

- Product number 1144032; the checked Japan page was set to `Chestnut`.
- Recycled ballistic-canvas mesh and suede upper, with a mesh vamp; webbing heel strap; oversized padded tongue.
- Chunky two-tone flat laces intended to be worn loose/unbound, plus a spare lace.
- Sugarcane-derived EVA outsole; approximately 3.2 cm heel height and 325 g per shoe.

### Actionable MOVERNO direction

Use as a **mid-low soft-volume sneaker** reference: broad padded tongue, wide ribbon-like lace field, tactile suede/mesh blocking and a light, modest platform. This opens a different proportion from R05's silver/white giant-rope chunky sneaker: lower visual center, broader flatter laces, softer collar/tongue volume, and matte material contrast rather than metallic technical bulk.

Proposed R11 brief: `padded ribbon-lace trail lounge sneaker` — stone-grey suede frame over smoky open mesh; a wide flat woven lace crossing an asymmetric MOVERNO webbing cage; slightly flared 3–3.5 cm foam/rubber unit; one offset heel pull and a shallow protective toe bumper. Keep the shoe mid-low and generous, not narrow, formal, orthopedic, or a conventional 2000s runner.

### Do not copy

- UGG wordmarks/logo patch, exact panel map, exact two-tone lace treatment, heel-webbing configuration, or outsole tooling.
- Do not reproduce the exact Chestnut tonal recipe; move to MOVERNO's cool mineral palette and proprietary asymmetric lace anchors.

### What the official page itself states

- Product code JR5758; colour `Clear Sky / Cloud White / Gum`.
- The page calls it a sporty, ballet-flat-inspired lifestyle shoe derived from an early-2000s design.
- Soft natural-leather upper, rubber outsole, contrasting leather side stripes and heel tab.
- Supplied with three pairs of laces in different lengths/materials, intended to enable different lacing looks.

### Why it was not selected

Main-agent review found the blue MEI too close to the already explored feminine ballet direction. Retain it only as evidence of a low-ground lace-wrap category; do not force it into R07–R12.

### Do not copy

- adidas three-stripe side branding, Trefoil/wordmarks, heel-tab branding, exact panel/seam layout, or the exact JR5758 lace arrangement.
- Do not add a fold-over tongue.
- Do not let the translation collapse into a generic Mary Jane strap, slim retro runner, or formal ballet flat. MOVERNO needs a proprietary asymmetric cord path and a visibly different sole break.
