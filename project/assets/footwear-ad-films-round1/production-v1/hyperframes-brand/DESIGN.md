# MOVERNO — Moving studies

## Style Prompt
Dark contemporary footwear cinema for young Asian audiences. Photorealistic, naturally grounded models and exact selected shoe designs carry the work. Graphite architecture, angled daylight, dry stone and reflective glass provide structure. Ivory rubber and genuine metallic accents remain legible. Product close-ups have controlled, slow movement; the human movement must come from actual generated motion clips, not pans over still portraits. Editorial typography is secondary to footwear.

## Colors
- #101110 — near-black end cards and deep negative space.
- #262a2a — architectural graphite.
- #ece6d9 — ivory typography, matching the rubber palette.
- #9c8154 — muted old-gold accent, used only sparingly.
- #c1b9ad — warmer secondary copy where contrast permits.

## Typography
Use the supplied original MOVERNO transparent wordmark asset, never redraw it. English labels: Arial, restrained tracking. Chinese single-line closing copy: Microsoft YaHei. No duplicate campaign slogans across shoes.

## Motion
30 fps, 1080×1920 delivery container, exactly 900 frames each. Free Wan source motion may be lower resolution and 16 fps; never describe it as native 1080p. HyperFrames makes a 4-second brand sting with staggered emblem/wordmark/line entrances, expo.out / power3.out / sine.inOut. Remotion assembles editorial product inserts and real model motion using short dissolves, preserving full shoes at contact moments. No stretching, shape morphs, spinning shoes, autonomous floating laces or decorative HUDs.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not substitute A03's later revision for the user's selected platform high-top.
- No competitor marks, copied ad footage, unlicensed music, invented product performance claims or AI-generation overlay text.
- No static portrait zoom passed off as walking or try-on movement.
- No neon grids, excessive gold ornaments, butterfly/X versions of the brand emblem.
- No purchasing, upgrading, paid APIs, account duplication or quota circumvention.

## Workflow boundary
Keep all new assets under production-v1. This creative media directory is not a Git repository, so no new worktree or commit is introduced. All original source images remain unchanged.
